Healthy Starts empowers people to make simple but meaningful health changes by taking Juice Plus+ and making other dietary and lifestyle changes.

What the
survey says

When families take part in Healthy Starts, real change begins to show:

  • 71% cut back on junk food and soft drinks
  • 71% drink more water
  • 61% eat more fruits and veggies
  • 60% miss fewer school days

Why Healthy Starts
matters

89% of respondents to our surveys tell us that Healthy Starts has made their young person more aware of their health and wellness overall**

When you participate in Healthy Starts, good things happen. Juice Plus+ is one simple change that can start you and your loved ones down the road toward developing other healthy habits like better nutrition, fitness, sleep, and hydration
